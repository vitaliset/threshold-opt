\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{General notation and conventions}}\\
\midrule
$\mathbf{1}$, $\mathbf{1}_K$; $\mathbf{I}$ & All-ones vector of $\mathbb{R}^K$; identity matrix & Sec.~\ref{sec:inference} \\
$p_k$, $[\mathbf{v}]_k$, $\boldsymbol{\mu}_j[k]$ & $k$-th entry of a vector: $p_k$ for a named vector $\mathbf{p}$; $[\mathbf{v}]_k$ for an expression such as $\mathbf{C}^\top\mathbf{p}$; $\boldsymbol{\mu}_j[k]$ for a vector whose subscript is a class index & Eq.~\eqref{eq:bayes_optimal_delta} \\
$\mathbb{I}(\cdot)$ & Indicator function, also written $\mathbb{I}\{\cdot\}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning} \\
$\arg\min$, $\arg\max$ & Minimizing (maximizing) index, with ties broken toward the smallest index; in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:thm_vertex_consistency}, \eqref{eq:dlc}, and~\eqref{eq:seqd2_invariant}, the set of minimizers & Eq.~\eqref{eq:bayes_optimal} \\
$\|\cdot\|_1$, $\|\cdot\|_\infty$, $\|\cdot\|_2$ & $L_1$, $L_\infty$, and Euclidean norms; $\|\cdot\|$ without subscript is Euclidean & Prop.~\ref{prop:excess_risk_bound} \\
$B^A$ & Set of all functions $A \to B$, as in $\{0,1\}^{\mathcal{X}}$ and $\mathcal{Y}^{\mathcal{X}}$ & Def.~\ref{def:vc_dimension} \\
$\mathrm{softmax}$ & Map $\mathbf{s} \mapsto \bigl(e^{s_k} / \sum_{j} e^{s_j}\bigr)_{k \in \mathcal{Y}}$ from $\mathbb{R}^K$ onto the interior of $\Delta^{K-1}$ & Eq.~\eqref{eq:dirichlet_calibration} \\
$\mathbf{e}_k$ & Vertex of $\Delta^{K-1}$ at which class $k$ has probability one & Sec.~\ref{sec:geometry} \\
$\mathrm{Dir}(\boldsymbol{\alpha})$ & Dirichlet distribution on $\Delta^{K-1}$ with concentration $\boldsymbol{\alpha} \in \mathbb{R}^K_{>0}$ (not the bias vector $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ of Sec.~\ref{sec:parallel_shifts}) & Sec.~\ref{sec:voronoi_samplers} \\
$\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{m}, \Sigma)$ & Gaussian distribution with mean $\mathbf{m}$ and covariance $\Sigma$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_variance} \\
$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mathbb{Q} \,\|\, \mathbb{Q}')$ & Kullback--Leibler divergence between distributions $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{Q}'$ on a common space & App.~\ref{app:kl_invariance} \\ % chktex 8

\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Spaces, random variables, and data}}\\
\midrule
$\mathcal{X} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ & Input feature space of dimension $d$, with Borel $\sigma$-algebra $\Sigma_{\mathcal{X}}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$\mathcal{Y} = \{0, \ldots, K{-}1\}$ & Label space, with discrete $\sigma$-algebra $\Sigma_{\mathcal{Y}}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$K$ & Number of classes & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$\mathbb{P}$ & Data-generating measure on $\mathcal{X} \times \mathcal{Y}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$(X, Y)$; $x$, $y$ & Random pair, $(X, Y) \sim \mathbb{P}$; realizations $x \in \mathcal{X}$, $y \in \mathcal{Y}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$\mathcal{S} = \{(x_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^N$ & Sample used to fit $\hat{\mathbf{p}}$ (inference stage); in the experiments of Chapters~\ref{sec:binary_experiments} and~\ref{sec:multiclass_experiments}, $N$ is the dataset size & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$\mathcal{V} = \{(x_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^m$ & Held-out validation set, disjoint from $\mathcal{S}$; training sample of the decision stage, of size $m$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning} \\
$\pi_y = \mathbb{P}(Y{=}y)$ & Class prior & Sec.~\ref{sec:binary_objectives} \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Probability estimators}}\\
\midrule
$\hat{\mathbf{p}} \colon \mathcal{X} \to \Delta^{K-1}$ & Probability estimator (function and, via $\hat{\mathbf{p}}(x)$, its output) & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$\Delta^{K-1}$ & Probability simplex $\{\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^K : \mathbf{p} \geq 0,\, \mathbf{1}^\top\mathbf{p} = 1\}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:inference} \\
$\mathbf{p}$ & Generic point of $\Delta^{K-1}$, such as the argument of a decision rule & Sec.~\ref{sec:inference} \\
$\boldsymbol{\eta}(x)$, $\eta_k(x)$ & Conditional class probability vector and its entries, $\eta_k(x) = \mathbb{P}(Y{=}k \mid X{=}x)$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:inference} \\
$Z = \hat{\mathbf{p}}(X)$, $z_i$ & Input of the decision stage, $z_i = \hat{\mathbf{p}}(x_i)$; a score $z \in [0,1]$ when $K = 2$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning} \\
$\hat p(x)$, $\eta(x)$ & Binary scalar reductions $\hat p_1(x)$, $\eta_1(x)$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:binary_setup} \\
$\boldsymbol{\epsilon}(x) = \hat{\mathbf{p}}(x) - \boldsymbol{\eta}(x)$ & Estimation error & Sec.~\ref{sec:cost_geometry} \\
$\Psi \colon \Delta^{K-1} \to \Delta^{K-1}$ & Systematic estimator distortion, $\hat{\mathbf{p}} \approx \Psi(\boldsymbol{\eta})$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:analytical_fragility} \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Costs and risk}}\\
\midrule
$\mathbf{C} \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^{K \times K}$ & Cost matrix; $C_{ij}$ is the cost of predicting $j$ when the true class is $i$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$C_{max}$ & Maximum entry of $\mathbf{C}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$R(h)$, $R(\delta)$ & Expected risk $\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{P}}[C_{Y, h(X)}]$ of a classifier $h$; for a decision rule, $R(\delta) = R(\delta \circ \hat{\mathbf{p}})$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning}) & Eq.~\eqref{eq:expected_risk} \\
$R^*$ & Bayes risk, $\inf_{h \in \mathcal{H}_{all}} R(h)$ & Eq.~\eqref{eq:bayes_risk_def} \\
$\mathcal{R}(\hat y \mid x) = \mathbf{c}_{\hat y}^\top \boldsymbol{\eta}(x)$ & Conditional risk of action $\hat y$ at $x$ & Eq.~\eqref{eq:conditional_risk} \\
$\mathbf{c}_i$ & $i$-th column of $\mathbf{C}$: costs of predicting class $i$ under each true class & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_risk} \\
$\hat R(\delta)$, $\hat R(\tau)$ & Empirical risk on $\mathcal{V}$ of a decision rule or of a binary threshold; $\hat R_{CV}$ is its cross-validated average & Eq.~\eqref{eq:decision_erm} \\
$\mathcal{L}_i(\mathbf{p}) = \mathbf{c}_i^\top \mathbf{p}$ & Conditional-risk linear functional; equals $\mathcal{R}(i \mid x)$ when $\mathbf{p} = \boldsymbol{\eta}(x)$ & Eq.~\eqref{eq:linear_risk} \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Classifiers, decision rules, and decision regions}}\\
\midrule
$h \colon \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{Y}$ & Classifier; decomposed as $h = \delta \circ \hat{\mathbf{p}}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$\delta \colon \Delta^{K-1} \to \mathcal{Y}$ & Decision rule & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$h^*$ & Bayes-optimal classifier & Eq.~\eqref{eq:bayes_optimal} \\
$\delta^*$ & Bayes-optimal decision rule, $\delta^*(\mathbf{p}) = \arg\min_{\hat y \in \mathcal{Y}} [\mathbf{C}^\top \mathbf{p}]_{\hat y}$, so that $h^* = \delta^* \circ \boldsymbol{\eta}$ & Eq.~\eqref{eq:bayes_optimal_delta} \\
$\mathcal{P}_i^*$ & Closed Bayes-optimal region of class $i$; differs from the assigned region $\mathcal{P}_i^{\delta^*}$ only on ties & Eq.~\eqref{eq:bayes_regions} \\
$\hat h$ & Plug-in classifier, $\hat h(x) = \delta^*(\hat{\mathbf{p}}(x))$ & Eq.~\eqref{argmin_risk} \\
$\mathcal{P}_i^\delta = \delta^{-1}(\{i\})$ & Decision region of class $i$ under $\delta$; the superscript may instead be the rule's parameter ($\mathcal{P}_i^{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}$, $\mathcal{P}_k^{\mathcal{M}}$) and is dropped when the rule is clear from context & Sec.~\ref{sec:geometry} \\
$\mathcal{B}_{ij}$ & Hyperplane $\{\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^K : (\mathbf{c}_i - \mathbf{c}_j)^\top \mathbf{p} = 0\}$ that contains the boundary between $\mathcal{P}_i^*$ and $\mathcal{P}_j^*$ & Thm.~\ref{thm:rigid_partition} \\
$\mathbf{n}_{ij} = \mathbf{c}_i - \mathbf{c}_j$ & Normal of $\mathcal{B}_{ij}$; for a Voronoi rule the normal is $\mathbf{n}_{vor} = \boldsymbol{\mu}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_j$ & Thm.~\ref{thm:rigid_partition} \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Generalization and capacity}}\\
\midrule
$\mathcal{H}_{all}$ & All measurable $h \colon \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{Y}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:problem_formulation} \\
$\mathcal{H}_{dec}$ & Hypothesis class of decision rules $\Delta^{K-1} \to \mathcal{Y}$ (context-dependent) & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning} \\
$d_{dec}$ & Capacity of $\mathcal{H}_{dec}$, or an integer upper bound on it: $d_{VC}$ for $K = 2$, $d_{Nat}$ for $K > 2$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning} \\
$d_{VC}$ & Vapnik--Chervonenkis dimension & Def.~\ref{def:vc_dimension} \\ % chktex 8
$\mathcal{H}_0$ & Countable class of measurable rules that approximates every rule of $\mathcal{H}_{dec}$ pointwise & Prop.~\ref{prop:gen_bound} \\
$\nu \in (0, 1]$ & Failure probability; the generalization bounds hold with probability at least $1 - \nu$ & Prop.~\ref{prop:gen_bound} \\
$c_{bin}$, $c_{Nat}$ & Factors of the generalization bounds, which depend on $m$ and $1/\nu$ (and, for $c_{Nat}$, on $K$ and the capacity bound $d$) through logarithms; $c$ in App.~\ref{app:complexity} stands for either & Eq.~\eqref{eq:clean_gen_bound} \\
$\epsilon > 0$ & Target generalization gap in sample-size conditions such as $m \geq c_{bin}^2 C_{max}^2 d_{dec} / \epsilon^2$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning} \\
$d_P$ & Pseudo-dimension (real-valued analogue of $d_{VC}$) & Def.~\ref{def:pseudo_dimension} \\
$W$ & Number of real-valued parameters of a class (not the Dirichlet matrix $\mathbf{W}$) & Prop.~\ref{prop:calibrator_complexity} \\
$d_{Nat}$ & Natarajan dimension (multiclass analogue of $d_{VC}$) & Def.~\ref{def:natarajan_dimension} \\
$s_k(\mathbf{p}; w)$ & Score of class $k$ in a rule $\mathbf{p} \mapsto \arg\min_k s_k(\mathbf{p}; w)$ with parameter $w$ & Thm.~\ref{thm:ps_complexity} \\
$\Phi_k$, $g_k$ & Fixed feature map and fixed parameter-free offset of a linear score $s_k = w^\top \Phi_k + g_k$ ($g_k$ is not the calibrator $g$) & Lem.~\ref{lem:linear_embedding} \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Binary thresholds}}\\
\midrule
$\tau \in [0,1]$ & Binary threshold & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning} \\
$\delta_\tau(z) = \mathbb{I}(z \geq \tau)$ & Binary threshold rule on a score $z \in [0,1]$, applied to $z = \hat p(x)$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:threshold_rule} & Sec.~\ref{sec:decision_learning} \\
$\mathcal{H}_{thresh}$ & Binary threshold indicators on $[0,1]$ & Lem.~\ref{lem:vc_thresh} \\
$p^*$ & Elkan analytical threshold & Eq.~\eqref{eq:elkan_threshold} \\
$\tau_{emp}$ & Threshold that minimizes the empirical risk on $\mathcal{V}$, or in Sec.~\ref{sec:cv_threshold} the cross-validated risk & Sec.~\ref{sec:empirical_threshold} \\
$\mathcal{T}$, $G$; $\hat\tau$ & Uniform grid of $G$ thresholds ($G = 301$ in the experiments); minimizer of $\hat R$ over the grid & Sec.~\ref{sec:threshold_grid_search} \\
$F$, $\mathcal{D}_{train}$ & Number of cross-validation folds; training data split into the folds & Sec.~\ref{sec:cv_threshold} \\

\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Multiclass partitions}}\\
\midrule
$\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times K}$, $\mathbf{a}_i$ & Learnable partition matrix and its $i$-th column & Eq.~\eqref{eq:obrien_rule} \\
$\delta_{\mathbf{A}}$ & Partition rule, $\delta_{\mathbf{A}}(\mathbf{p}) = \arg\min_{i \in \mathcal{Y}} \mathbf{a}_i^\top \mathbf{p}$ & Eq.~\eqref{eq:obrien_rule} \\
$\mathcal{H}_{Full}$ & Partition rules $\delta_{\mathbf{A}}$ with unconstrained $\mathbf{A}$; $K^2$ free parameters & Eq.~\eqref{eq:hfull_def} \\
$\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ & Parallel-shift bias vector, $A_{ij} = C_{ij} + \alpha_j$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:parallel_shifts} \\
$\boldsymbol{\beta}$ & Linear-scaling weights, one per true class, $A_{ij} = C_{ij}\beta_i$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:linear_scaling} \\
$\boldsymbol{\tau}$ & Per-class threshold vector: in $\mathbb{R}^K_{>0}$ with $\tau_0 = 1$ for probability-ratio thresholding, in $\mathbb{R}^K$ for multidimensional thresholding, in $[0,1]^K$ for sequential selection & Eq.~\eqref{eq:threshold_ratio} \\
$\mathcal{H}_{Sc}$ & Linear-scaling partition class, equal under $0/1$ loss to probability-ratio thresholding; $K{-}1$ free parameters & Eq.~\eqref{eq:hsc_def} \\
$\mathbf{v}$ & Parallel-shift offset vector with $\sum_k v_k = 0$ (the bias $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ or the threshold $\boldsymbol{\tau}$ in a common gauge) & Eq.~\eqref{eq:hps_def} \\
$\mathcal{H}_{PS}$ & Parallel-shift partition class, equal under $0/1$ loss to multidimensional thresholding; $K{-}1$ free parameters & Eq.~\eqref{eq:hps_def} \\
$\mathrm{ord}$ & Class evaluation order (a permutation of $\mathcal{Y}$) for sequential selection & Sec.~\ref{sec:sequential_selection} \\

\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Voronoi partitions}}\\
\midrule
$\boldsymbol{\mu}_k \in \mathbb{R}^K$ & Voronoi prototype (anchor) of class $k$; in $\Delta^{K-1}$ for the simplex-restricted class & Sec.~\ref{sec:voronoi_rule} \\
$\mathcal{M} = (\boldsymbol{\mu}_0, \ldots, \boldsymbol{\mu}_{K-1})$ & Prototype configuration, an ordered $K$-tuple whose prototypes need not be distinct & Sec.~\ref{sec:voronoi_rule} \\
$\delta_{\mathcal{M}}$ & Voronoi decision rule, $\delta_{\mathcal{M}}(\mathbf{p}) = \arg\min_{k \in \mathcal{Y}} \|\mathbf{p} - \boldsymbol{\mu}_k\|_2$ & Def.~\ref{def:voronoi_rule} \\
$\mathcal{H}_{Vor}$ & Voronoi rules with prototypes in $\mathbb{R}^K$; $K^2$ free parameters; equal to $\mathcal{H}_{Full}$ (Prop.~\ref{prop:voronoi_full_equality}) & Sec.~\ref{sec:voronoi_rule} \\
$\mathcal{H}_{Vor}^{\Delta}$ & Voronoi rules with prototypes in $\Delta^{K-1}$; $K(K{-}1)$ free parameters & Sec.~\ref{sec:voronoi_rule} \\
$\mathcal{H}_{Vor}^{\mathrm{DLC}}$ & Subclass of $\mathcal{H}_{Vor}^{\Delta}$ that satisfies the dominant label constraint & Def.~\ref{def:dlc} \\
$d^2(\cdot, \cdot)$ & Squared Euclidean distance; at a vertex, $d^2(\mathbf{e}_k, \mathbf{p}) = 1 - 2p_k + \|\mathbf{p}\|_2^2$ for $\mathbf{p} \in \Delta^{K-1}$ & Eq.~\eqref{eq:dsq_identity} \\
$M$ & Retry budget of the retry-based samplers ($M = 500$ in the experiments) & Sec.~\ref{sec:voronoi_samplers} \\

\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Misspecification-sensitivity analysis (Chapter~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_K})}}\\
\midrule
$\mathbb{P}_\lambda$, $\mathbb{P}_\xi$ & Members of the variance-scaling ($\lambda \geq 1$) and mean-shift ($\xi \geq 0$) families; the reference members are $\mathbb{P}_1$ and $\mathbb{P}_0$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_experiments} \\
$\mathbf{m}_k \in \mathbb{R}^d$ & Class-conditional Gaussian mean in feature space ($d = 2$ for variance scaling, $d = K{-}1$ for mean shifts) & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_variance} \\
$\sigma > 0$ & Class-conditional Gaussian scale (not the logistic sigmoid) & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_variance} \\
$\lambda \geq 1$ & Variance multiplier; $\mathbb{P}_\lambda$ has covariance $\lambda\sigma^2\mathbf{I}$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_variance} \\
$R_\lambda$, $h^*_\lambda$; $R_\xi$, $h^*_\xi$ & Expected risk under $\mathbb{P}_\lambda$ (or $\mathbb{P}_\xi$) and its Bayes-optimal classifier; $h^*_1$ (or $h^*_0$) is the misspecified classifier & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_variance} \\
$R_{\mathrm{circ}}(K)$ & Circumradius of the equidistant class-mean configuration & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_meanshift} \\
$\xi$ & Per-class Euclidean mean-shift magnitude & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_meanshift} \\
$\phi_\delta(\mathbf{p}) = \mathcal{L}_{\delta(\mathbf{p})}(\mathbf{p})$ & Per-sample expected cost of rule $\delta$ at $\mathbf{p}$ & Eq.~\eqref{eq:per_sample_cost_functional} \\
$\mathbb{P}_\theta$, $\mathbb{P}_{\mathrm{ref}}$ & Perturbed member ($\theta = \lambda$ or $\theta = \xi$) and reference member of either family & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_lemma} \\
$\boldsymbol{\eta}_{\mathrm{ref}}$, $\boldsymbol{\eta}_\theta$ & Conditional class probabilities under $\mathbb{P}_{\mathrm{ref}}$ and $\mathbb{P}_\theta$ (written $\boldsymbol{\eta}_1$, $\boldsymbol{\eta}_\lambda$ and $\boldsymbol{\eta}_0$, $\boldsymbol{\eta}_\xi$ in Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_experiments}) & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_lemma} \\
$V = \{\mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^K : \mathbf{1}^\top\mathbf{v} = 0\}$ & Tangent space of admissible probability-vector perturbations & Eq.~\eqref{eq:perturbation_space} \\
$\boldsymbol{\epsilon}$, $r$ & Perturbation in $V$ and its Euclidean radius $r = \|\boldsymbol{\epsilon}\|$; cf.\ the estimation error $\boldsymbol{\epsilon}(x)$ & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_lemma} \\
$\mathbf{g}$; $\rho$, $M$ & Gradient in $V$ of a scalar functional of $\mathbf{p}$; smoothness radius and remainder constant (not the retry budget $M$) & Sec.~\ref{sec:misspec_sensitivity_lemma} \\

Errors do not cost the same

A classifier's output triggers an action, and its two kinds of error rarely cost the same.

Medical diagnosisA missed disease, against an unnecessary biopsy or operation.
Fraud detectionA fraud that goes through, against a blocked legitimate payment.
Industrial fault monitoringAn undetected fault, against an unnecessary stop.

A false negative often costs orders of magnitude more than a false positive, and the default rule, which predicts the most probable class, treats them as equal.

  • Three settings where a missed positive costs far more than a false alarm.
  • The default rule ignores the costs; the talk is about what to use in its place.

A threshold in binary, argmax in multiclass

Two classes

scikit-learn tunes the threshold $\tau$ on held-out data with TunedThresholdClassifierCV.

Three classes

The rule is a partition of the probability triangle, with no single number to tune, and argmax is the usual choice.

This dissertation asks how to parametrize the multiclass partition and how to learn it from held-out data.

  • In binary, the decision is one threshold, and tuning it is common practice.
  • Three probabilities that sum to $1$ lie on a triangle in space; a rule is a partition of it, with no single knob, so practitioners keep argmax. Drag the figure to turn it.
  • How to parametrize that search is what started this work.

Contributions

The decision rule is a component to be learned from held-out data.

  1. A common framework for multiclass decision rules. We write published multiclass rules as partition matrices and bound the capacity of each class.
  2. Voronoi prototypes for the partition. The rule assigns each input to the nearest of $K$ prototypes. On a constructed distribution it has strictly lower risk than every shift of the Bayes boundaries. We also give a sampler for them.
  3. A benchmark of decision strategies. 14 binary and 11 multiclass datasets, 10 classifiers, 10 binary and 12 multiclass objectives.
  4. Sensitivity to the number of classes. Relative to the best achievable risk, the untuned rule loses less as the number of classes grows.
  5. An open-source library. The threshold_opt library, available on PyPI, implements every strategy and the benchmark.
  • Five contributions; a slide that presents one carries its name in the title bar.
  • The first third of the talk is the setting they build on.
